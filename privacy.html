Privacy Policy — Pi YouTube Upload

Last updated: November 25, 2026

What this app does

Pi YouTube Upload is a personal tool used only by its owner. It uploads videos the owner created to the owner's own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API v3, and reads basic channel information to confirm the upload destination.

What data is accessed

The app uses the following Google API scopes, only after the owner signs in with Google and grants permission:

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload) — to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly) — to read the owner's channel information.

No other Google data is accessed.

How data is used and stored

Video files, titles, descriptions and tags are sent to YouTube only to publish the owner's videos. Access tokens are stored securely on the owner's own server and are used only for these uploads. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access immediately stops the app from reaching the YouTube account.
